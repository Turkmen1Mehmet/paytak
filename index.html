Support

For any question, bug report or suggestion about Paytak, e-mail us at t44mehmet63@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

How to play

  • Tap: hop forward
  • Swipe: left, right or back
  • Don't get hit by cars, step on the logs in the river. Don't dawdle — the screen keeps moving!

FAQ

No sound. The game is silent while the device's silent switch is on; turn it off and raise the volume.

Where is my best score stored? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Do I need internet? No, the game works fully offline.
